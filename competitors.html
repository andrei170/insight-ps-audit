The hub page is the bit you are missing

Dalys has a page for each town on its own, as well as a page for each service in that town. That town page acts as a hub — it catches somebody searching just "driveways Chorlton", and it passes authority down to the twelve service pages beneath it.

Your structure goes straight to /block-paving/oldham with nothing sitting above it for Oldham as a place. You have the harder half built and are missing the easy half. Sixty-one extra pages would close it.

Also worth seeing: they reach past Greater Manchester — Crewe, Nantwich, Warrington. Whether that is a market you want is a business question, not a website one.

The 45 Guides
This is where the enquiry actually starts

Nobody wakes up searching "block paving Oldham". They start weeks earlier, searching things like how much does a resin driveway cost, block paving vs tarmac, why is my driveway sinking, or do I need planning permission for a driveway.

Dalys has 45 pages positioned for that stage. You have none. So the person doing their homework meets them first, and by the time they are searching for a paver in a named town they have already been reading a competitor's answers for a fortnight.

This is the cheapest gap on the page to close, and it plays directly to twenty-five years of knowing why driveways fail — which is content you can produce and most firms cannot.

Where You Can Actually Win
You are much closer to the front of this market than you probably think
  • You have matched a firm trading since 1969 on structural coverage. 732 town pages against 721. Most of this market is not remotely in that conversation — the next firm we checked had six.
  • Your structured data is better than theirs. You publish LocalBusiness markup, geo-coordinates, opening hours, a price range and a real FAQ block. That is work most competitors have not done.
  • Nobody in this set has a careers page. You told us staff is the constraint that is stopping you taking on more work. The channel that would fix it is empty for everyone.
  • Nobody in this set sells roofing. You already say you do. That is a genuine differentiator in a market where every competitor is a driveway-only firm — and it is currently invisible because you have no roofing pages.
  • Ground-to-roof is a real positioning advantage. One contractor, one visit, one set of insurance, for a homeowner who needs a drive and a roof seen to. None of these firms can say it.
  • Fourteen years of trade-body history. Dalys has longevity you cannot buy; you have more of it than any other firm here, and it is under-used on the site.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Actual search rankings. No rank tracker has been run against any firm here. Page counts are structural evidence, not a ranking report, and a bigger site does not automatically outrank a smaller one.
  • Review counts and ratings. No competitor's review count or rating is stated anywhere in this document, and we did not audit anyone's trade profiles including yours.
  • Two firms returned no sitemap to us — Pave Resin Ltd and JM Driveways Manchester. That means we could not count their pages. It is not evidence that they are small, and they were left out of the table rather than guessed at.
  • Analytics on competitor sites. We found no tracking code in the HTML served to us for any firm, but modern frameworks can load measurement in ways an outside check will not see. We make no claim about what any competitor measures.
  • The quality of anyone's town pages, yours included. We counted the structure. We did not grade the writing on 732 pages, and thin town pages can hurt rather than help.
  • Google Business Profiles and the map pack. Not assessed for any firm. In this trade that often produces more enquiries than the website does.
  • Whether these firms advertise vacancies elsewhere. We checked their own websites only.

732 pages against 721. You are not behind on effort.

You are behind on the town hub pages, the guides, and knowing which of it works.

Finding 1 — You Sell Roofing And You Have No Roofing Pages
Your homepage says, in its own words: "Our Roofing, Paving and Groundworks Work" "Comprehensive Roofing Services" "our skills extend from the ground to the roof" Your own structured data tells Google what kind of business you are: "mainEntityOfPage": "RoofingContractor" Now here is every service you have actually built pages for: artificial-grass-installation indian-sandstone-patios block-paving paving-repairs-and-maintenance brickwork-and-garden-walls resin-bound-driveways driveways-and-landscaping tarmac-driveways garden-design turfing-and-lawns garden-fencing groundworks-and-site-preparation Pages whose address contains the word "roof": 0 Pages for garden fencing: 61
This is the single biggest gap on the site

You have told Google you are a roofing contractor. You tell visitors you do roofing in the first heading on the page. And you have built sixty-one pages about garden fencing and none at all about roofing.

Roofing is typically a higher-value job than a fence. If it is genuinely a service you want, the machine you have already built will produce roofing pages the same way it produced fencing ones — the structure exists, the service is simply missing from it.

Why this is the expensive one

You have sixty-one towns. Some of them will be producing enquiries and some will be producing nothing. Right now those two categories look identical to you.

It also means that if you ever advertise — Google, Meta, anything — you would be spending blind from day one. No advertising should start before this is fixed, and fixing it is a short job, not a project.

This is the prerequisite for everything else in this document.

Finding 3 — Your Reputation May Be Filed Under A Different Name
Fourteen years of Checkatrade, and a six-month-old name

You told our team you have been with Checkatrade for about fourteen years. That is a serious asset — it is the thing a homeowner checks before they let you on the drive.

Two things we found that are worth a conversation:

  • Insight PS Limited was incorporated on 6 March 2025 — six months ago. Your site says over twenty-five years of experience. Both can be true, and usually are: a long-standing trader who incorporated recently. But the name "Insight PS" is new.
  • The trade-body link on your own website points to a profile under a different trading name — the address ends insight-blockpaving-driveways, not Insight PS.

If fourteen years of reviews sit under one name and the website is building another, the trust does not transfer automatically. Somebody who searches "Insight PS reviews" may find very little, while the actual record is under the older name.

Not assessed: we could not load the trade profile itself, so we make no claim about how many reviews exist or what they say. This is a question to answer, not a finding to act on blind.

Finding 4 — Nothing On The Site Speaks To The Problem You Named
Pages on insightps.co.uk about joining the team: careers ......... 0 vacancies ....... 0 jobs ............ 0 work for us ..... 0
You said the constraint is staff, not enquiries

You told our team you need more people so you can take on more jobs. That makes recruitment the binding constraint — and there is nothing on a 747-page website aimed at a groundworker or a paver deciding where to work next.

You already own the machine that would fix this. The same structure that generated sixty-one town pages per service will generate a careers page and town-level hiring pages. It is the cheapest possible use of infrastructure you have already paid for.

What Is Genuinely Good Here
So this does not read as a list of complaints
  • The town-page structure is real and most of your competitors will not have one. Twelve services by sixty-one towns is a serious build.
  • Your structured data is properly done. LocalBusiness markup, geo-coordinates, opening hours, a price range and a genuine FAQ block with real questions in it. That is better than most trade sites we look at.
  • Checkatrade and TrustMark are both referenced. Those are exactly the signals this market responds to.
  • The site is fast and clean — which is partly because nothing is loaded onto it. Adding analytics properly will not undo that.
  • Over twenty-five years of experience and fourteen years of trade-body history. That is the hardest thing on this page to buy and you already have it.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Install analytics and conversion tracking this week. Nothing else on this list can be judged until you can see. This is days, not weeks.
  • Wait a month and read it. Find out which of the sixty-one towns actually produce enquiries. Our guess is that a small number do most of the work — but it would be a guess, and you would be paying us to stop guessing.
  • Settle the name question. Decide whether Insight PS or the older trading name carries the reputation, and make the website, the trade profiles and the Google listing agree.
  • Build the roofing pages — if roofing is genuinely a service you want. The structure already exists; this is populating it, not inventing it.
  • Build one careers page and point local traffic at it. Staff is your stated constraint. Recruitment advertising is cheap in this trade because almost nobody bids on it.
  • Only then consider paid advertising. With tracking in place and the best towns known, paid traffic becomes a decision rather than a gamble.
